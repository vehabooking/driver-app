Veha Booking Driver is the official app for transport drivers registered with a Veha Booking operator. It helps you manage the private and airport transfer trips your operator assigns to you, from pickup to drop-off.

ASSIGNED TRIPS
• See today's and upcoming trips assigned by your operator
• Full booking details: pickup point, destination, time and passenger info

PICKUP & NAVIGATION
• Route map to the pickup and the destination
• Open directions in your preferred maps app
• Call the passenger with one tap

TRIP PROGRESS
• Move each trip through clear steps: Start, Arrived, Meet Passenger, Drop Passenger
• Report a pickup issue if the passenger cannot be found

LIVE TRIP TRACKING
• During an active trip, your location is shared with dispatch and the passenger so they know when you will arrive
• Tracking runs only while a trip is in progress and stops when the trip ends

MORE
• Trip notifications
• English and Khmer
• Light and dark mode

An account is required. Driver accounts are created by your Veha Booking operator; contact your operator for access.
